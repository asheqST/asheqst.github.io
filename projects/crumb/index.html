Crumb

Overview

A browser extension that answers cookie consent banners with the most privacy-preserving choice, automatically.

Technologies

PrivacyBrowser extensionTypeScript

Problem and motivation

Consent banners are designed to make accepting easy and refusing tedious. Most people click “Accept all” just to reach the page.

Approach

Crumb recognizes banners from the major consent platforms, as well as generic ones, and chooses “Reject all” or “Necessary only.” When refusal is buried in a settings panel, it works through the multi-step flow on the user’s behalf.

Technical design considerations

  • Privacy as the default. The most protective option is chosen unless the user decides otherwise.
  • Transparent. An optional activity log shows exactly what was selected on each site.
  • In the user’s control. It can be switched off globally or for individual sites.

Privacy and security

  • Minimal permissions. The extension asks only for what it needs.
  • No telemetry. Nothing about browsing is collected or sent anywhere.
  • No remote code. Behavior ships with the extension and cannot be changed from a server.

Outlook

Privacy regulation gave people a choice; interface design often takes it back. Small tools that exercise that choice automatically restore it at scale.

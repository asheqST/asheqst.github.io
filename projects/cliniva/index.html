Cliniva Healthcare

Overview

A cloud healthcare platform with telehealth, co-founded and led from concept to adoption by more than 500 physicians and 10,000 patients.

Technologies

Product leadershipHealthcareTelehealthCloud

Problem and motivation

Physicians needed a practical way to work with patients and consult remotely, with patient data handled securely. In healthcare, trust in how data is handled is a precondition for adoption.

Approach

Cliniva gave physicians one platform for working with their patients, including video consultations, on web and mobile. The product was built and delivered by a small team using Agile planning, automated testing, and continuous delivery, and it grew to more than 500 physicians and 10,000 patients.

Technical design considerations

  • Security as a product feature. Protecting patient data was part of the product, not a compliance afterthought.
  • Delivery discipline. A small team shipped reliably through planning, testing, and automation.
  • Designed with users. Interface decisions were validated through usability testing.

Security considerations

  • Access by role. Each user saw only what their role allowed.
  • Secure transactions. Authentication and payments were built to protect patient and financial data.
  • Aligned with healthcare norms. Logging and privacy controls followed HIPAA and PCI DSS principles.

Outlook

Building Cliniva showed that in healthcare, security and usability are not trade-offs: both are what earn adoption. That lesson shapes the security work that followed.

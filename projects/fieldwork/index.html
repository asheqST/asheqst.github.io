ProductIndependent project

Fieldwork

Overview

A training simulator for forward-deployed engineers: investigate a simulated customer case, make engineering decisions, test them against a deterministic engine, and get a debrief.

Technologies

SimulationNext.jsTypeScriptPlaywright

Problem and motivation

Forward-deployed engineers learn their most important skills on the job, in front of real customers, with real consequences. There is little structured practice for working with imperfect evidence under time pressure.

Approach

Fieldwork places learners in a simulated customer environment. They investigate incomplete evidence, make engineering decisions, run those decisions against a deterministic simulator, inspect the trace, and receive a specific debrief. The first scenario covers a supplier’s invoice-intake workflow.

Technical design considerations

  • Practice, not lectures. Learning happens by making decisions and seeing their consequences.
  • Deterministic outcomes. The same decision always produces the same result, which makes feedback fair and reviewable.
  • Synthetic by design. Every company and record in the simulation is fictional.

Trust and safety

  • No real customer data. Scenarios use synthetic data only, so the product can be shared freely.
  • Hardened delivery. Strict browser security policies and rate-limited sign-in protect learners and the platform.
  • Content is checked. Scenario content is validated automatically before every build.

Outlook

As more engineering roles sit directly with customers, the ability to diagnose messy situations becomes a core skill. Simulation makes that skill teachable and measurable.

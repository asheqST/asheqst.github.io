Background

TPSAT was a third-party risk assessment project undertaken during my cybersecurity internship at ProMedica Health System.

The project addressed third-party cybersecurity risk within a healthcare organizational context, where external technology providers and service relationships can introduce additional security, operational, and compliance considerations.

Problem and motivation

Healthcare organizations depend on a wide ecosystem of vendors, technology platforms, service providers, and external business partners. These relationships can introduce risks involving:

  • Sensitive information exposure
  • External access to organizational systems
  • Vendor cybersecurity posture
  • Technology dependencies
  • Data protection
  • Service continuity
  • Regulatory obligations

Third-party risk assessment helps organizations evaluate these risks in a structured manner and support informed security and governance decisions.

Assessment considerations

In general, a third-party risk assessment weighs:

  • Vendor security posture and the evaluation of its security controls
  • Access to sensitive information and to internal systems
  • Risk identification and prioritization, supported by assessment evidence
  • Regulatory considerations that apply to the relationship
  • Remediation of identified gaps, and ongoing visibility as relationships change

Healthcare context

Healthcare organizations hold some of the most sensitive information there is, run services where downtime affects patient care, and connect to a large number of external systems. A weakness at a vendor can become a path into the organization or a source of data exposure. HIPAA, PCI DSS, data-protection obligations, and organizational risk management all shape how these relationships are evaluated and governed.

Confidentiality

TPSAT was undertaken in a professional healthcare cybersecurity environment. Organization-specific implementation details, vendor information, assessment findings, and internal security documentation are not publicly available.
